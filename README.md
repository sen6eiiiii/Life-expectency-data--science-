# Life-expectency-data--science-
A data science project exploring global life expectancy data. The project focuses on data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and identifying key health and socioeconomic factors associated with life expectancy across countries and years using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.
# Life Expectancy Data Science

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores global life expectancy data using data science techniques. It was completed as a **group project at university**, giving us the opportunity to work collaboratively on data preparation, analysis, visualisation, and interpretation.

The project investigates life expectancy across different countries and years, while exploring how health, economic, and social factors may be associated with differences in life expectancy.

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Clean and prepare the life expectancy dataset.
* Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA).
* Identify trends and patterns in life expectancy.
* Explore relationships between life expectancy and other health and socioeconomic factors.
* Create meaningful data visualisations.
* Interpret the results and communicate our findings.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains information about life expectancy and various health, economic, and social indicators for countries across multiple years.

The analysis looks at variables that may contribute to differences in life expectancy between countries.

## 🛠️ Technologies & Tools

* **Python**
* **Pandas** – Data manipulation and analysis
* **NumPy** – Numerical computing
* **Matplotlib** – Data visualisation
* **Seaborn** – Statistical data visualisation
* **Jupyter Notebook** – Analysis and documentation

## 🔎 Project Process

Our group followed a typical data science workflow:

1. **Data Collection** – Examined and imported the provided dataset.
2. **Data Cleaning** – Checked for missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis** – Investigated distributions, trends, and relationships within the data.
4. **Data Visualisation** – Created graphs and charts to communicate key findings.
5. **Analysis** – Examined factors associated with differences in life expectancy.
6. **Conclusion** – Summarised our findings and discussed the insights gained from the analysis.

## 🤝 Group Project

This project was completed collaboratively as part of a **university data science assignment**. Working as a group allowed us to divide tasks, share ideas, review each other's work, and develop our understanding of the data science process.

The project also helped us develop skills in **teamwork, communication, problem-solving, data analysis, and presenting technical findings**.
